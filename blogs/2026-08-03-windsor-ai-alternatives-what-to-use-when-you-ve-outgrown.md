The best Windsor.ai alternatives are Funnel, Supermetrics, Coupler.io, AgencyAnalytics and Whatagraph. Funnel is the strongest choice for marketers who have outgrown basic extraction and need a marketing data hub that natively stores and models data. The other alternatives fill lighter roles and range from pure data pipelines to automated client reporting dashboards

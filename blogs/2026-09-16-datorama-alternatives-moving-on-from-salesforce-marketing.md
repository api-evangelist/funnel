The best Datorama (Marketing Cloud Intelligence) alternatives are Funnel, Adverity, Improvado and Fivetran. Funnel is the closest like-for-like replacement and stays independent of Salesforce. Adverity and Fivetran suit engineering-led teams building into a warehouse.

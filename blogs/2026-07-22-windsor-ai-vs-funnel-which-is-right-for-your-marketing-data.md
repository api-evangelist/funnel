Windsor.ai is a data connector that extracts marketing metrics from ad platforms and moves them to a destination. Funnel is a marketing data hub that collects, stores, prepares, contextualizes and shares data in a central system before moving it onward to a report. While Windsor.ai focuses on data transfer and attribution, Funnel provides a data foundation with native storage and advanced data transformation.

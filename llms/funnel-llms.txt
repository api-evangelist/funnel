# Funnel

> Funnel is a marketing intelligence platform that gives brands and agencies dependable marketing data everywhere decisions are made. It brings marketing data together from any source, stores it, applies consistent definitions and keeps history intact, so reporting, business intelligence, measurement and AI all run on the same trusted data. Funnel is delivered through two products: Data Hub, the core platform that collects, stores, prepares and activates marketing data and includes built-in dashboards and Funnel AI, and Funnel Measure, an add-on for measurement such as marketing mix modeling, multi-touch attribution and incrementality testing.

# Funnel

> Funnel (commonly known as funnel.io) is the leading Marketing Intelligence solution in the market. It helps marketers from agencies and brands alike, by integrating with hundreds of data platforms in order to streamline data analysis. It offers data collection, data visualization and advanced marketing measurement solutions (MMM and MTA) to thousands of brands all around the world.
